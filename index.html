About the app

Taking a moment each day to notice the good things in your life can make a real difference. Gratitude Journal makes that habit easy. Open the app, answer a short daily prompt, write up to three things you're grateful for, and pick how you're feeling. It takes about a minute.

Over time, your entries build into a personal record you can look back on, with streaks and simple insights that show your progress.

Features

Everything you need for a daily gratitude practice, and nothing you don't.

Three good things a day

Write up to three things you're grateful for each day, with a fresh prompt to help you get started.

Mood tracking

Record how you feel in one tap: Awful, Bad, Okay, Good or Amazing.

Streaks

See your current streak, your best streak and your total entries right on the home screen.

History calendar

Browse past months on a calendar, tap any day to reread it, and edit or delete entries.

Insights

Completion rate, monthly activity, your most active journaling days, mood distribution and your most common words.

Light and dark themes

Choose Light, Dark or follow your phone's system setting.

Easy editing

Update any entry, or delete it with an undo option in case you change your mind.

You're in control

Delete a single entry at any time, or clear all of your data from Settings.

How it works

Three simple steps, once a day.

  1. Tap the plus buttonOpen today's page from the home screen.
  2. Write and pick a moodUse the daily prompt for ideas, add up to three things, then choose how you feel.
  3. Save and look backWatch your streak grow and revisit past days in History and Insights.

Your privacy

  • Your entries are stored on your own device.
  • The app does not use the internet and needs no account.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • Android's built-in backup can include your entries if you have it turned on.
