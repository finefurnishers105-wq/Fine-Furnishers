Privacy Policy

Gratitude Journal is a private, offline journal. Your entries stay on your device. The app has no account, no internet access, no ads and no analytics.

Effective date: November 17, 2026 Last updated: November 17, 2026

Stored on your device

Entries are saved in the app's private storage on your phone.

No internet access

The app does not request the internet permission and cannot send data online.

No account needed

There is no sign-up, login or profile. We never ask for your name or email.

No ads or trackers

No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party SDKs.

On this page

    01Introduction

    This Privacy Policy explains how the Gratitude Journal Android application (the "App") handles information. The App is published by FINE FURNISHERS AND FOAM HOUSE (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED ("we", "us" or "our").

    Gratitude Journal helps you write down up to three things you are grateful for each day, record how you feel, look back on past days in a calendar, and see your streaks and simple insights. The App was built to work entirely on your device. We do not operate servers for the App, and we do not receive, collect or store any of your information.

    By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

    02Information the App Stores on Your Device

    The App only stores the information you choose to enter, plus one display setting. All of it is saved locally in the App's private storage on your device and is never sent to us.

    Journal entries

    Up to three gratitude notes for each day, the date of the entry, the time it was last saved, and an optional mood (Awful, Bad, Okay, Good or Amazing).

    Theme preference

    Whether you chose Light, Dark or System default for the App's appearance.

    Information we do not collect

    The App does not collect, access or request:

    • Your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details
    • Your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone
    • Device identifiers, advertising IDs or IP addresses
    • Usage statistics, analytics or behavioral data
    • Payment or financial information

    The streaks, completion rate, mood distribution and "most common words" shown in the App are calculated on your device from your own entries. They are never transmitted anywhere.

    03App Permissions

    The App does not request any permissions that require your approval. In particular, it does not request access to the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, notifications or accounts.

    Android build tools automatically add one internal permission that only lets components inside the App communicate with each other securely. It does not give the App access to any of your data or to other apps.

    04How Information Is Used

    The information stored on your device is used only to make the App work for you:

    • To show, edit and organize your journal entries
    • To display your history calendar and past entries
    • To calculate your streaks and insights on your device
    • To apply the theme you selected

    We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, marketing or any other purpose, because we never receive it.

    05Device Backups and Transfers

    The App supports Android's built-in backup and device-transfer features. This means your journal entries and theme preference can be copied when you:

    • Use Google backup: if backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the App's data in your device backup to your Google Account.
    • Move to a new device: when you transfer data from an old phone to a new one, the App's data may be copied to the new device.

    These features are provided and controlled by Google and Android, not by us. Backed-up data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy, and we cannot access it. You can turn device backup on or off at any time in your device's Settings.

    06Data Sharing and Disclosure

    We do not sell, rent, trade or share your information with anyone. The App has no way to send your journal entries to us or to third parties.

    Some options in the App's Settings screen open other apps on your device, only when you tap them:

    Actions that open other apps
    OptionWhat happens
    Rate usOpens the App's page in the Google Play Store app, or in your web browser if Google Play is not available.
    Share appOpens Android's share menu with a short message and a link to the App on Google Play. Your journal entries are never included. You choose which app, if any, receives the message.
    Privacy policyOpens this policy in your web browser.

    Once another app is opened, its own privacy policy applies to anything you do there.

    We may disclose information if required by law. However, because we do not hold any of your information, we would have nothing to provide.

    07Third-Party Services

    The App does not include any third-party SDKs or services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, authentication, cloud storage, payments or artificial intelligence. It does not use Firebase, Google Sign-In, AdMob or any similar service. The App is built only with standard Android and Google Material Design libraries, and these work entirely on your device.

    Google Play

    The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update apps, and if you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your device, Android may send crash reports to Google. Google may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as install counts and crash rates. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and none of it includes your journal entries.

    08Data Retention and Deletion

    Your entries stay on your device until you delete them. You are always in control:

    • Delete a single entry using the delete icon on its card on the Home screen, in the entry editor, or from the History screen.
    • Delete everything using Settings → Clear all data. This permanently removes every journal entry from the App.
    • Remove all App data by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

    Deleted entries cannot be recovered by us. Copies kept in a Google device backup follow Google's retention rules and can be managed from your Google Account and device settings.

    09Data Security

    Your entries are stored in the App's private storage, which Android isolates so that other apps cannot read it. Because the App has no internet access, your data is never transmitted over a network by the App.

    The App does not add its own encryption layer. Your entries are protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's built-in storage encryption. To keep your journal private, we recommend using a screen lock such as a PIN, pattern, password or biometric unlock, and keeping your device's software up to date.

    10Children's Privacy

    The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Because no information leaves the device, we do not knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions, please contact us using the details below.

    11Your Rights and Choices

    Depending on where you live, you may have rights under data protection laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information.

    Because all of your information stays on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the App at any time: view and edit your entries, delete individual entries, or clear all data. We do not hold copies of your information, so we cannot access, correct, export or delete it on your behalf. You can still contact us with any privacy question.

    12Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App's features change. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how information is handled, we will make it clear in the App's store listing or in an App update. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
